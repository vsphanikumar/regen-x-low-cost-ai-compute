# Problem statement

This project addresses a practical question: how can flexible AI workloads be routed to times and places where renewable energy is available in surplus, without forcing the system to operate as a rigid always-on data-center footprint?

REGEN-X models this as a local simulation and product demo that combines grid telemetry, workload constraints, pricing signals, and compliance-style reporting. The goal is to make the concept concrete in a way that can be reviewed and discussed without requiring a live energy-operations stack.
