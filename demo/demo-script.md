# Demo script

This page is intended to hold the live walkthrough and public-facing demo narrative for REGEN-X.

Suggested flow:

1. Open the dashboard and identify a renewable corridor with curtailment and negative price conditions.
2. Explain the simulated grid conditions and why the workload is an attractive candidate for routing.
3. Trigger the orchestration flow and review the decision output.
4. Inspect the generated Green Compute Passport and compliance ledger.
5. Summarize the value proposition and the operational assumptions behind the demo.
